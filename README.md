# Comparative Study: Work Shifts & Impact on Hospital Operations

**Organization:** Dr. R. B. Patil Multispecialty Hospital, Hubli  
**Research Focus:** Impact of shift patterns on employee stress and operational effectiveness

## 🎯 Problem

A 150-person hospital's shift scheduling system doesn't align with actual workload patterns—creating unnecessary employee stress and patient-safety risk.

## 📊 Methodology

- **Sample:** 137 employees (stratified sampling from 150-person population)
- **Departments:** Emergency, Surgery, Cardiology, Orthopedics, Gynecology
- **Parameters Analyzed:** 
  - Work pressure
  - Mental stress
  - Sleep disruption
  - Fatigue levels
  - Work-life balance
  - Coordination effectiveness

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Night shifts: 3x higher stress/fatigue than day shifts
- Rotational shifts: Worst coordination, highest fatigue accumulation
- Shift transitions: 25% drop in coordination effectiveness
- Root cause: Circadian disruption + staffing velocity mismatch + poor handoffs

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Velocity-Based Scheduling** - Align staff to actual workload patterns by hour
2. **Fatigue Management Program** - Sleep education, recovery protocols, health monitoring
3. **Structured Handover Protocol** - Documented, structured shift transitions
4. **Operating Model Redesign** - Long-term shift structure improvements

## 📈 Expected Impact

- 20-25% reduction in employee stress
- 10-15% improvement in patient safety incidents
- Improved staff retention in night shift roles
- Better cross-departmental coordination

## 📁 Project Deliverables

- Full research report with findings
- Root cause analysis
- Business case for recommendations
- Implementation roadmap (phased rollout)
- Success metrics and tracking
